# ⛳🏈 Football Trivia Golf

Guess the player at a given spot on an NFL career leaderboard ("Who is #23 all-time in rushing yards?").
Strokes = 1 + how many spots away your guess is; off-list guesses cost 12. Par 4 per hole, 9 holes, lowest score wins.

- **Daily course**: same 9 holes for everyone each day — compare with friends.
- **Random round**: new holes every time.
- **Pass & play**: up to 8 golfers on one device.

Questions live in `data.js` (career totals through the 2025 season). Add a category by appending to `categories`.
